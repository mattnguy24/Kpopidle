Welcome to Kpopidle, a daily K-Pop guessing game!

Kpopidle is a daily guessing game where you need to guess the idol with 8 tries! Use the hints available and view the silhouette if you need extra help! Goodluck! Idol will change everyday at 12 AM PST. For questions or inquiries, contact kpopidleguessing@gmail.com

Rules

  • You have 8 tries to guess the mystery idol!
  • If you need extra help, feel free to view the idol's silhouette.
  • If the idol you guessed has a similar feature, a green check ✅ will be shown.
  • If the age or debut is yellow 🟡, that means your guess is within 2 years!
  • If height is yellow 🟡, that means your guess is within 5 cm!
  • Idol will be updated everyday at 12 AM PST!
